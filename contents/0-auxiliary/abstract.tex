With the rise of zero-knowledge technologies and their applications in fields such as blockchain and anonymous identity management, it is essential to develop a comprehensive understanding of the underlying mechanisms.
However, existing resources on the topic tend to be either too high-level or too low-level, making it hard for practicing engineers to grasp the practical implications of zero-knowledge protocols.

This book aims to bridge this gap by providing a complete, practical guide to state-of-the-art techniques in zero-knowledge cryptography, such as $\Sigma$-protocols, Bulletproofs, popular ZK-SNARKs like Groth16 and PlonK, sum-check-based protocols and lookup arguments, and even post-quantum schemes, including ZK-STARK and the lattice-based LaBRADOR.
We have gathered all the necessary information in one place and tried to make it easy to follow, with numerous examples and code snippets.
Each chapter comes with exercises to help you understand the material better.
Despite the book's practical focus, we preserve mathematical rigor where suitable and necessary.
